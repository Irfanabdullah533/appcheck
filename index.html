Browser Share & Phone Install

How "Share → WordVault" Works

When you highlight a word and its definition in Chrome or Google Search and tap Share, your phone sends the text to WordVault via URL parameters (?text=...) and automatically splits the word from its meaning.

1. Host Your App Free (30 Seconds)

To appear in Android's native Share menu, the app needs a real HTTPS link. Upload the downloadable wordvault-pwa.zip folder to a free static host like Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages.

2. Install to Home Screen

  • Android (Chrome): Open your hosted link → tap ⋮ Menu → Add to Home screen / Install app. WordVault immediately appears in your system Share sheet!
  • iPhone (Safari): Open your hosted link → tap the Share icon → Add to Home Screen.
